## Business communication
![Evidence](https://github.com/Keeuwbuck/Digital_Portfolio2/blob/main/business%20comms.png)

#### Situation
I have been assigned to work on a group project. The group members were randomly picked students so nobody knew each other. In the beginning of the project the communication between us was very bad and inconsitant among us. Group members were to shy to ask for any help because they were unsure of their work.

#### Task
I had to step in and be the project leader. I created schedules for meetings, allocated task to each member and made sure our deadline was met.My goal as leader was  to create an atmosphere where my team members felt comfortable to communicate with each other.

#### Action
I improved our groups communication by implementing 5 minutes for each person to talk and give us more information about their tasks assigned to the them. This helped to reduce any pressure of speaking infront of each other and it build their confidence to speak up. I also ask them to give a chance to explain their progress to the team . After each meeting I schedule 10 minutes of their time just to speak to each other and learn more about each other.

#### Result
As 2 weeks went by the communication of our group significantly improved. Members that were hesitant to speak began to speak more freely and engaged more with other members. 


## Interview skills
![Evidence](https://github.com/Keeuwbuck/Digital_Portfolio2/blob/main/interview%20skills.png)

#### Situation
As part of my career development i had to participate in a mock interview video. This video prepared me alot and gave me alot of confidence to attend  real interviews. I recently had an interview with a company and it went surprisingly good. I talked with confidence and answered the question with confidence. Having the mock interview gave me the ability to attend the interview with confidence.

#### Task
The goal was to maintain  good body language, eye contact and use professional tone throughout the whole interview. 

#### Action
To prepare myself I researched interview questions I could potentially face in the interview. This allowed me to have well structured answers and answer any question with confidence. I took deep breaths in between just to regroup and continue to be positive.

#### Result
The interviewer gave me positive feedback to improve for future interviews. The interview went well but there is alot of key aspects I still need to improve for my next interview. For example i have to do research about the company and know they history as well. This gave me a clear idea of what to expect for interview in the future.

## CWorkplace Etiquette
![Evidence](https://github.com/Keeuwbuck/Digital_Portfolio2/blob/main/workplace.png)
#### Situation
During my internship at my company I realised that people come late to work without facing any consequences. I also realised during lunch time they are doing the same. This created negative views for some of the workers.

#### Task
Although these things were happening in work place I tried to be professional at all times. I respected the work hours, arrived early for work and returned early to the office during our lunch time.

#### Action
I reminded some of the workers about our work hours. I encourage them to respect the work hours and to try to arrive early as possible. This is the reason I always to to act professional to set an example to my fellow workers

#### Result
My supervisor started to recognize my performance and stated that he is pleased with my performances since I follow the companies polocies. My colleagues started to see what is happening and they started to adjust their times and they started to come earlier to work.

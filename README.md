# Digital_Portfolio2

## Business communication
![Evidence](https://github.com/Keeuwbuck/Digital_Portfolio2/blob/main/business%20comms.png)

#### Situation
I have been assigned to work on a group project. The group members were randomly picked students so nobody knew each other. In the beginning of the project the communication between us was very bad and inconsitant among us. Group members were to shy to ask for any help because they were unsure of their work.

#### Task
I had to step in and be the project leader. I created schedules for meetings, allocated task to each member and made sure our deadline was met.My goal as leader was trying to create an atmosphere for my fellow members to become comfortable to communicate with each other.

#### Action
I improved our groups communication between each other by implementing 5 minutes for each person to talk and give us more information about their tasks assigned to the them. This helped to reduce any pressure of speaking in fornt of each other and it build their confidence to speak up. I also ask them to give a chance to explain their progress for the team . After each meeting I schedule 10 minutes of their time just to speak to each other and learn more about each other.

#### Result
As 2 weeks went by the communication our group significantly improved. Members that were hesitant to speak began to speak more freely and engaged more with other members.
